# Runner OS — Product Vision & North Star

Status: product blueprint baseline. Documentation is not implementation evidence.

## North Star
Runner OS helps a runner make better running decisions consistently by connecting goals, real schedule, training history, activities, events, preferences and trusted integrations in one personal operating system.

The key outcome: the runner can answer, with evidence, “What should I do next, why is it right for me, and what changed after I did it?”

Success is not the number of agents, prompts, integrations or dashboard widgets. Success is clarity, consistency, preparation and reflection without fabricated facts or loss of user control.

## Product promise
- One coherent place for running life: profile, schedule, training, activities, events, integrations and decisions.
- Personal recommendations grounded in actual data and stated constraints.
- Clear separation of facts, plans, hypotheses, suggestions and confirmed outcomes.
- An agentic assistant that plans and coordinates work but asks permission before consequential changes.
- Useful operation when AI or third-party integrations are unavailable.

## Audience and launch boundary
Primary: recreational runner seeking structure, continuity, accountability and context-aware preparation. Initial deployment is optimized for one owner, while all personal records remain owner-scoped and multi-account-ready.

Future users receive isolated accounts, memory, settings, consent and integrations. Multi-user readiness does not imply public social features.

## Product pillars
1. Personal context: profile, goals, constraints, routine, availability and confirmed feedback.
2. Training OS: sessions, schedule, attendance, plans, progress and reflections.
3. Activity truth: manual and authorized imported activities with provenance and deduplication.
4. Event intelligence: race discovery, verification, intent, deadlines and preparation.
5. Agentic intelligence: orchestrated specialist capabilities, recommendations, drafts, approvals and review.
6. Integration fabric: Strava first, other adapters only after value is demonstrated.
7. Trust and control: privacy, ownership, consent, explainability, export, deletion and audit.

## Product principles
- Personalization before automation.
- Data truth before model fluency.
- Structured records before embeddings.
- One orchestrator before a swarm.
- Deterministic code before an LLM call.
- Read-only integrations before write access.
- Explicit consent before external access.
- User confirmation before canonical mutation.
- Graceful degradation before hard dependency.
- Measure usefulness and safety before expanding scope.

## Experience loop
Capture → Understand → Decide → Plan → Approve → Do → Observe → Reflect → Adapt.

Every recommendation must state its evidence, time window, assumptions and unknowns. Derived patterns remain hypotheses until validated or confirmed. The system must never infer medical diagnoses or sensitive traits.

## Explicit non-goals
No medical diagnosis or treatment, social network, public leaderboard, race registration bot, payments, messaging, Strava write-back, unrestricted agents, proprietary foundation model or mandatory Strava connection.

## North Star metrics
- Weekly meaningful use, not raw opens.
- Approved plan follow-through, with unknown outcomes kept unknown.
- Recommendation usefulness, including edited and rejected suggestions.
- Data completeness with provenance, not fabricated completion.
- Sync reliability and duplicate-free imports.
- Time to understand the next useful action.
- Zero unauthorized mutations and zero cross-owner data leaks.

## Release principle
MVP is the smallest trustworthy running OS, not a miniature of every future feature. Never market live Strava, agentic execution, predictive readiness or proactive automation until their acceptance gates pass.
